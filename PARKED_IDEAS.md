### 🥈 AutoDocs — AI Engineering Knowledge Capture

**Source:** r/managers · Score: 7.82 · WTP: High
**Quote:** *"They say it slows them down"* (devs refusing to document)

**Pain:** Senior devs hoard knowledge in their heads.

**Buyer:** Engineering Directors / Heads of Eng at IT services
**Contract size:** ₹20/engineer/month

**When to revive:** After AdminGuard validated + cross-sell motion proven.

### 🥉 PolicyLens — HR Layoff/Performance Fairness Tool

**Source:** r/Accounting · Score: 7.54 · WTP: High
**Quote:** *"PE is ruining the culture"*

**Pain:** Mass layoffs at Grant Thornton, Coinbase, etc. with
unclear criteria → lawsuits, attrition spikes, Glassdoor damage.

**Product:** AI that scores layoff/promotion decisions for

**Buyer:** CHRO / Head of HR Compliance / Legal at IT services
**Contract size:** ₹15-40L/year per company
**Indian angle:** Layoffs = court cases in India. Tool = insurance.

**When to revive:** When HR-tech becomes the buyer cluster post-AdminGuard.

## 🏙️ FixMyCity — Civic Infrastructure Accountability

**Source:** r/delhi · Opportunity Score: 7.22/10
**Quote:** *"Die today because you walked into some manhole"*

### Vision
A citizen platform where people report broken urban infrastructure
(manholes, water leaks, garbage, dead streetlights, potholes) via
photo + GPS, and the platform publicly ranks local officials by
their response time and fix rate.

Think: Trustpilot for cities + Waze crowdsourcing + accountability
scoreboards of named officials.

### Why Park It
- Universe-denting but slow to monetize
- Needs team, capital, government navigation
- Long validation cycles
- High burnout risk for solo founder

### When To Revive
- After GSTFlow hits ₹3L+ MRR
- Or if someone with civic-tech experience wants to co-found
- Or if a foundation (Omidyar, BMGF) shows interest
- Or after 1 year of building real founder muscle

### Why It's Real
- 2B+ people in dysfunctional cities globally
- Existing solutions (Swachh Bharat, Janaagraha) are weak
- Real lives at stake (the manhole quote is a death)
- Foundation funding exists in this space
